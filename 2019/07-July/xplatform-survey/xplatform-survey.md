We at Microsoft are constantly reaching out for your feedback so we can build
the software that works best for you. Right now, we are investigating an
opportunity of a cross-platform UI framework and would love to hear your
thoughts on this matter. Is it something we should invest in or should we
prioritize other stacks instead? If your application runs on different OSes, we
want to know how you implement it now and what solutions would you like to have
in future? Would your application benefit from a cross-platform UI framework? Or
is web - the direction you are thinking of in future?

This short [survey][survey] takes only 3 minutes and will help us significantly to
understand the demand and desires of our developers.  Make sure your voice is
heard so we can build the software that best suits you!
