App of the week: ShareX
-----------------------

[ShareX](https://getsharex.com/) is an astonishingly complete screen capture tool that supports both still and video capture, and upload to popular file sharing services. The best thing about it however is that [it's free, open source, and written in C#](https://github.com/ShareX/ShareX).
