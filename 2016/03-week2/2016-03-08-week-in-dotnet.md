Project of the week: Humanitarian Toolbox's allReady
----------------------------------------------------

[allReady](https://github.com/HTBox/allReady) is a project managed by the charity organization [Humanitarian Toolbox](http://www.htbox.org/) that is designed to assist the management of community preparedness campaigns. It's being tested by the [American Red Cross](http://www.redcross.org/) on a campaign to install smoke alarms within homes in the Chicago area. The project is built with ASP.NET Core and Entity Framework 7. It is also open source.

<a href="http://www.htbox.org/projects/allready"><img src="http://www.htbox.org/upload/projects/allready/allreadybiglogo.png" alt="allReady" style="width:500px;max-width:100%"/></a>

This is a [great opportunity to get your feet wet with open source and .NET Core](http://stevejgordon.co.uk/contributing-to-allready), while contributing to great humanitarian causes. Please head over to [the allReady project's home page](http://www.htbox.org/projects/allready) to learn more.
