Model Explainability ensures your machine learning models can be debugged or audited. By understanding how and why your model reacts in certain situations, you can ensure reliability and robustness, while avoiding bias.

Tell us about how you want to interpret your models and assess bias in [ML.NET](https://dot.net/ml) by taking this ~10 minute survey.

At the end, you can optionally leave your contact information if you’d like to talk with the ML.NET team about your Model Explainability and Fairness feedback.
