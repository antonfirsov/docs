Project of the week: nopCommerce
--------------------------------

[nopCommerce](http://www.nopcommerce.com/) is a popular open-source e-commerce system built on ASP.NET MVC, Autofac, and Entity Framework. It's been downloaded 1.8 million times, has more than a hundred partners, and is used by popular brands such as Volvo, BMW, Puma, Reebok, Lacoste, and many more.
