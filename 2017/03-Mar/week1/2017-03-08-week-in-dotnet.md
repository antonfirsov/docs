Tool of the week: FNA
---------------------

[FNA](http://fna-xna.github.io/) is a reimplementation of the [Microsoft XNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_XNA) libraries. The main contributor to the project, [Ethan Lee](http://www.flibitijibibo.com/), has [ported two dozen XNA games](http://www.flibitijibibo.com/index.php?page=Portfolio/Ports) already, including FEZ, Bastion, and Terraria.

Game of the week: Pyre
----------------------

[Pyre](https://www.supergiantgames.com/games/pyre/) is a role-playing game in which you lead a band of exiles to freedom. To do this, you must fight through ancient competitions that are spread across a mystical purgatory. Each battle will bring you and your party closer to their freedom as they gain access to new abilities. Pyre will feature both a campaign and a two-player versus mode, letting you challenge a friend to one of fast-paced ritual showdowns.

![Pyre](pyre.jpg)

[Pyre](https://www.supergiantgames.com/games/pyre/) is being created by [Supergiant Games](https://www.supergiantgames.com/) using [C#](https://channel9.msdn.com/Series/C-Sharp-Fundamentals-Development-for-Absolute-Beginners) and their own custom engine. It is under development but will be launching on [Steam](http://store.steampowered.com/app/462770/) and PlayStation 4.
