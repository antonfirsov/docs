This year was packed full of amazing releases, conferences, and .NET developer content. The [.NET YouTube](https://youtube.com/@dotnet) reached new heights by adding nearly 60K new subscribers bringing the total over 200K! Thank you all so much for being [subscribers](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCvtT19MZW8dq5Wwfu6B0oxw?sub_confirmation=1), joining in on discussions, and jamming that like button on all of that great content that the team and community puts out. We have seen over 7 million videos views which amounts to over 650K hours consumed! That is over 27,000 days or 3,870 weeks, or 74 years of watch time! With some fun stats out of the way, let's get into the top videos of 2022.

## Top .NET videos of 2022

Let's start things off with the top new on-demand videos that were released this year. This list does include sessions from [.NET Conf](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8V_BUGFKdaI&list=PLdo4fOcmZ0oVlqu_V8EXUDDnPsYwemxjn&index=2), but not the live streams themselves.

### [Clean Architecture with ASP.NET Core 7](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j6u7Pw6dyUw)

Steve Smith's presentations on Clean Architecture continue to take the top spot on views each year form .NET COnf. This year Steve looks at clean architecture (aka Onion, Hexagonal, Ports-and-Adapters) to help organize your code in a way that limits its dependencies on infrastructure concerns in ASP.NET Core.

## Top .NET Live Streams of 2022

Some of the most popular content on the .NET YouTube are live streams from the .NET teams where they engage directly with you! You can always find the latest upcoming streams on the .NET website's [https://live.dot.net](.NET Live TV) page. 

### .NET Conf

.NET Conf [Day 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sMOGPRbsbcs) and [Day 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IkL8rJDuB4U) took top spots as you may have guessed, but [.NET Conf: Focus on .NET MAUI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vc22hoUorzA) and the [.NET Conf 2022 - Student Zone](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9pbyBGnw8p0) were not that far behind.

### [Learn C# with CSharpFritz](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/live/csharpfritz)

Each monday, Jeff Fritz live codes, answer questions, and builds fun .NET projects. This year he had tons of top live streams, but [Get Started with ASP.NET Core MVC](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LnfZ23an4eA) took the top spot! Be sure to watch it back and binge on his back catalog.

### [.NET 20th Anniversary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=67tCWKnweso)

The [Let's Learn .NET series](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/live/lets-learn-dotnet) introduces developers to different .NET topics and .NET MAUI took the top spot.
